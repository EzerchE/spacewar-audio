# Legacy maintenance policy

Archived publicly on 28 September 2026. No further fixes, compatibility updates,
issue triage or support are promised. Source, tags and existing release assets
are retained; none are a recommendation for current ROMs.

Reason: this was a device/ROM-specific workaround. Recent limited testing on
the maintainer's updated Spacewar succeeded with the module disabled. Reusing
its historical mixer/policy overlays on newer firmware can introduce regressions.
Long-term, cross-device and all-call-path reliability have not been established.

Do not interpret archive status as proof that the original ROM bugs are fixed
everywhere. Users of older affected builds must evaluate their own compatibility
and recovery plan. Repository archival does not toggle, uninstall or remotely
update anything on a phone.

Daily Profile and Meta Earbud Background are separate projects with different
purposes; neither is an audio-fix replacement.
